What stays

Staff keep moderation records, such as cases, notes, reports and voice actions, so nobody can wipe the evidence about themselves. Notes and reports end when staff delete them. Cases stay on record, and staff can void one. To ask about one, talk to the server's staff. Public posts, like a suggestion or a vote on a proposal, stay with the server's record of them.
