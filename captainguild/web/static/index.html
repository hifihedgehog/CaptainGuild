Captain Guild

One bot for moderation, roles, logs, reports, custom commands and posts, run from a web dashboard on your own server. The code is open and the data stays yours.

Open the dashboard

What it does

Moderation

Bans, kicks, mutes, timeouts, warnings and notes, each one a numbered case you can search, edit and export. Actions taken outside the bot are recorded too.

Automod

Spam, links, invites, mentions, caps, bad words and honeypot channels, with your choice of punishment per rule and a dry run to test a rule first.

Logging

Message, member, server and voice events in the channels you pick, naming who did it, with a search page for anything logged.

Reports

Members report a message or a person from the right-click menu. Staff get the evidence, the history and a checklist in one thread.

Reaction roles

Roles from reactions, buttons or menus, with limits, one-only groups, verify gates and timed roles. Edit any of it at any time.

Embed editor

Write posts with a live preview, edit them later, go back to any earlier version, schedule them, and bring over posts from other bots.

Tags and triggers

Custom commands and automatic replies in a small template language with variables, math, conditions and embeds.

Voice controls

Temporary voice mutes and deafens, voice access requests with requirements, and a trusted tier for short mutes with an optional vote.

Suggestions and proposals

Members suggest and vote, and staff decide. A bigger change goes to two votes on one post, one by members and one by moderators, and it passes only if both pass.

Greetings and levels

Welcome and goodbye messages, welcome images with layouts, uploaded backgrounds and a layer designer, birthdays, XP levels with rank cards and reward roles, starboard and sticky messages.

Your data

Everything lives in encrypted databases on the machine you run it on. You can export a server's whole setup and history as one file, or put it back, from the dashboard. Nothing is sold, shared or used to train anything.
