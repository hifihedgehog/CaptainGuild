# Guild Courier terms

Guild Courier is free software under the license of the Captain Guild repository it ships in. It comes with no warranty.

It is meant for one use: sending the public posts of Reddit accounts you name to a Discord webhook you control, so a Discord bot can relay them. Use it only for accounts whose posts you may repost, and follow Reddit's rules and Discord's rules when you do.

Whoever installs the app is responsible for what it sends and where. The app's authors run no service and hold none of its data.

Problems and questions go to https://github.com/hifihedgehog/CaptainGuild/issues.
