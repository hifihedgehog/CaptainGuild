# Guild Courier privacy policy

Guild Courier is installed and run by one person, on a Reddit community of their own.

## What it reads

The public posts of the Reddit accounts its installer names in its settings: each post's ID, title, author, community, link, time, body and the addresses of its pictures or video. It reads no comments, no private messages and nothing about the people who visit the community it is installed on.

## What it sends, and where

Each new post it reads is sent to one Discord webhook, the delivery address its installer set. It is sent nowhere else.

## What it stores

For each followed account, the IDs and post times of the last 300 posts it noted or sent, and the time it first looked at the account. It also keeps the time of its last hourly log line. All of it sits in the storage Reddit gives the app for the community it is installed on. It stores no post's title, body or pictures.

## Contact

Questions go to the person who installed the app, or to the issue tracker at https://github.com/hifihedgehog/CaptainGuild/issues.
